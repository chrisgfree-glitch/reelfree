About ReelFree

ReelFree is a hand-picked guide to movies and TV shows you can watch free and legally. We don't host, upload, copy or proxy any video. Every title plays on its original source, either inside an official embedded player or on the source's own site.

Where the titles come from

  • Internet Archive (archive.org): public-domain films and TV episodes, played through the Archive's own embedded player.
  • Official YouTube channels: full movies posted by the studios and distributors that own them (for example Popcornflix and FilmRise), played through YouTube's embedded player. We don't list random re-uploads.
  • Tubi and Pluto TV: free, ad-supported streaming services. We link straight to the title's page on their site, where it plays with their ads.

How we keep links working

An automatic checker tests every title every night from a US server. Archive items are confirmed through the Archive's public metadata, YouTube videos through YouTube's public embed lookup, and Tubi and Pluto TV pages by loading them and confirming the title is still there. If a title fails two nights in a row, it's hidden automatically until it works again. Availability can differ outside the US.

Found a problem? Every title page has a Report broken link button. Reports are anonymous; we don't ask for your email.

Accounts

When accounts are switched on, favorites and lists are private to you. There are no public profiles, comments or social features.

Rights

Titles, artwork and trademarks belong to their owners. ReelFree isn't affiliated with the Internet Archive, YouTube, Tubi or Pluto TV. If you own the rights to something listed here and believe it shouldn't be, use the report button on that title and we'll review it.
